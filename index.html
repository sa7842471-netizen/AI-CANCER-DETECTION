Project Overview

Solving India's Cancer Diagnostic Crisis

India diagnosed 1.46 million new cancer cases in 2022 with only 2,000 trained oncologists for 1.4 billion people. This platform bridges that gap.

🔬

Single-Cancer Tools

Existing AI tools (DeepMind, IBM Watson) are limited to one cancer type, causing fragmented workflows and missed multi-organ screenings.

Limitation
🏥

Image-Only Systems

Current clinical AI requires high-quality CT/MRI scans from hospital radiology — excluding patients in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

Limitation
🔒

Black Box Models

Most deep learning tools provide no explanations for predictions, a major barrier to clinical adoption and regulatory compliance.

Limitation
✅

Our Solution

Unified platform: 9 cancers, dual-input (image + text), Grad-CAM/LIME explainability, AI-IDS security, and LLaMA-3.1-70B chatbot.

Proposed System

Core Objectives

01
Unified AI Platform

Integrate CNN, EfficientNet-B0, ResNet50, DenseNet121 & VGG19 into one web-based application.

02
Cloud-Ready Deployment

Containerized via Docker with independent microservice scaling per cancer model.

03
Explainable AI (XAI)

Grad-CAM heatmaps and LIME explanations for every prediction to improve clinician trust.

04
DPDPA 2023 Compliance

AI-IDS, OTP auth, JWT tokens, AES-256 encryption and RBAC for full data security.

05
NLP AI Chatbot

LLaMA-3.1-70B-based 24/7 patient support with medical report analysis capabilities.

Detection Engine

9 Cancer Types Detected

Each cancer type has an independently trained, purpose-optimized deep learning model with dedicated preprocessing pipelines.

System Design

Five-Layer System Architecture

Client-server architecture with three primary tiers: Next.js frontend, Flask backend API, and Firebase data layer.

🛡️

Multi-Layer Security Architecture

🤖
AI-Based IDS

Scapy + TensorFlow classifier monitors real-time network traffic and classifies packets as benign or malicious.

🔐
OTP + JWT Auth

Two-factor OTP authentication with JWT session tokens expiring after 30 minutes of inactivity.

🗄️
AES-256 Encryption

Firebase Encryption SDK protects all stored patient data with AES-256 encryption at rest.

👥
RBAC

Role-based access control ensures patients and specialists access only their authorized data.

Development Approach

Agile-Scrum Methodology — 6 Phases

Development is broken into focused sprints, each delivering a specific testable component with sprint review cycles and continuous integration.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Every prediction includes interpretable evidence to build clinician trust and meet regulatory requirements.

Grad-CAM

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping generates visual heatmaps highlighting cancer-indicative regions in the final convolutional layer.

LIME

Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations perturb superpixels to identify which image regions most influenced the model's decision.
